**Full description**

Bubble Match-3 is a relaxed match-3 puzzle you can play anywhere, no internet needed.

Swap neighbouring bubbles to line up three or more of a colour. Match four for a Line bubble, an L or T shape for a Burst, and five in a row for a Prism that clears a whole colour. Chain cascades, then use your spare moves for extra stars.

★ 64 hand-made levels: score goals, ice, stone, moss that spreads, locked bubbles and cherries to bring down
★ Boosters: Hammer, Free switch, Row blast, Colour brush and +5 Moves, plus one Undo per level
★ Daily reward calendar (7 days) and a new Daily challenge every day
★ Skin Shop: bubble finishes and backdrops. Tap any skin to preview it, or try one free for 24 hours
★ Achievements and a Stats page to track your progress
★ A short tutorial on level 1 and a How to play card
★ Accessibility: reduce motion, larger text, high contrast, colour symbols on every bubble
★ Fully offline, small download

Fair play: coins are earned only by playing. They can never be bought or cashed out, and there are no loot boxes. Ads are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, plus an occasional ad between levels after your first session. Never on launch.
